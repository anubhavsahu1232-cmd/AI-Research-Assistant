**Abstract**  
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an increasingly influential component of modern educational practice, promising to personalize learning, streamline administrative tasks, and enhance assessment processes. This paper reviews the current state of AI applications in education, highlighting three primary domains: adaptive learning systems, intelligent tutoring systems, and educational analytics. In adaptive learning, AI algorithms adjust content difficulty and pacing to individual learner profiles, potentially improving engagement and academic outcomes; however, evidence for longâterm efficacy remains limited and requires further empirical validation. Intelligent tutoring systems, which emulate oneâonâone instruction through natural language processing and knowledge representation, have shown promising results in skill acquisition, yet their scalability and cultural adaptability are still under investigation. Educational analytics, leveraging large datasets from learning management systems, can uncover patterns in student behavior and inform instructional design; the ethical implications of data privacy and algorithmic bias must be carefully addressed. The review identifies gaps in longitudinal research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and transparent reporting standards. Future research directions include rigorous experimental designs to evaluate AIâenhanced interventions, crossâcultural studies to assess transferability, and frameworks for responsible AI deployment in educational contexts.
